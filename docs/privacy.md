# Foldertone privacy policy

Foldertone does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. It has no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking, and it makes no requests to servers operated by its developer.

## What stays on your device

Foldertone stores the following only inside its private app storage:

- Permission to read the folders and files you choose in Files.
- A list of those sources, the names you give them, and the selected playlist.
- A playback copy of each audio file, its embedded cover art, and its metadata, such as title, artist, and album.

Foldertone reads your original files and never changes or deletes them. Disconnecting a source, or deleting the app, removes its local data and leaves the originals in Files.

Playback copies and the track index are excluded from iCloud Backup. Other app settings may be included in your device backup under your own Apple Account settings.

## Other services

If you choose music stored in iCloud Drive or another Files provider, that provider delivers the file to your device under its own privacy policy. Foldertone receives only the files you select.

## Children

Foldertone does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes and contact

This policy will be updated here if the app's handling of data changes. For questions, use the [Foldertone support page](https://wiltodelta.com/projects/foldertone/support/).
